About Career Family Achievement

Cillian Murphy maintains a grounded, notoriously private family life with his wife, Irish visual artist Yvonne McGuinness. The couple met backstage at a Super Furry Animals concert in 1996 long before either could have anticipated their future paths and were married in 2004. Together, they have two sons: Malachy, born in 2005, and Aran, born in 2007. Raised in a quiet environment in Cork, Ireland, Murphy intentionally relocated his family from London back to Dublin during his Peaky Blinders years to give his children a normal Irish upbringing away from the Hollywood spotlight. Murphy consistently guards his domestic life with quiet intensity, bringing total exposure to his art while keeping what matters most completely sheltered from it.
