Cillian
Murphy

An Actor
Without Equal

Cillian Murphy was born on 25 May 1976 in Douglas, a suburb of Cork City, Ireland. Raised in a household that valued literature and music, he studied law briefly at University College Cork before the theatre claimed him entirely.

His stage work with Corcadorca Theatre Company in the late 1990s earned him critical acclaim before he transitioned to film. The 2002 Danny Boyle horror landmark 28 Days Later announced him to the world a gaunt, magnetic presence capable of devastating vulnerability.

His range has encompassed war heroes, crime lords, scientists, and spectres of guilt. In 2024, he became the first Irish actor to win the Academy Award for Best Actor, for his portrayal of J. Robert Oppenheimer.
