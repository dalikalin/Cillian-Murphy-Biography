Career

Cillian Murphy's multi-faceted career spans acting, radio curation, and film production, defined by a dedication to artistic depth across various mediums. Beyond his critically acclaimed performances on stage, television, and screen, he has shared his passion for music as a presenter on BBC Radio 6 Music and expanded his creative impact behind the camera as a film and television producer. His diverse body of work reflects a lifelong commitment to storytelling, compelling music selection, and supporting visionary artistic projects.

Actor

Murphy has spent over two decades delivering compelling performances across theater, film, and television. He achieved early international fame in Danny Boyle's 28 Days Later and went on to tackle a wide variety of roles from the iconic gangster Tommy Shelby in BBC's Peaky Blinders to frequent collaborations with director Christopher Nolan.

Radio Presenter

Since 2013, Murphy has hosted Cillian Murphy's Limited Edition (and periodic guest slots) on BBC Radio 6 Music, curating diverse playlists spanning jazz, blues, rock, and ambient music.

Film & Television Producer

He has taken on executive producer and producer roles for projects he has starred in, including Peaky Blinders, Small Things Like These (2024), Steve (2025), and Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man (2026). 

Screen Writer

Early in his film career, Murphy co-wrote the 2001 short film Watchmen.

Voice Actor & Voice Narrator

He has lent his voice to documentaries (such as the 2015 BBC series Atlantic: The Wildest Ocean on Earth and Waveriders), animated features (like Kensuke's Kingdom), and video games.
